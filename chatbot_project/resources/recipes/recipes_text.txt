Biryani

Ingredients:
Basmati rice 2 cups
Mutton or chicken 500g
Onion 3 large
Yogurt 1 cup
Ginger-garlic paste 2 tbsp
Bay leaves 2
Cinnamon stick 1
Cardamom 4
Cloves 3
Ghee 4 tbsp
Green chili 2
Mint leaves 1/4 cup
Salt to taste

Instructions:
Soak basmati rice for 30 minutes
Marinate meat in yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, and spices for 1 hour
In a heavy-bottomed pot, layer marinated meat and partially cooked rice
Add bay leaves, cinnamon, cardamom, and cloves between layers
Sprinkle ghee and mint leaves
Cover with foil and place lid on top
Cook on high heat for 2 minutes until steam forms, then cook on medium heat for 45 minutes
Let rest for 5 minutes before opening
Serve hot with raita and salad

Butter Chicken

Ingredients:
Chicken 600g
Yogurt 1 cup
Tomato puree 1.5 cups
Cream 3/4 cup
Butter 4 tbsp
Ginger-garlic paste 2 tbsp
Garam masala 1.5 tsp
Chili powder 1 tsp
Turmeric 1/2 tsp
Fenugreek leaves 2 tbsp
Oil 2 tbsp
Salt to taste

Instructions:
Marinate chicken in yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, and spices for 30 minutes
Heat oil in a pan and cook chicken pieces until golden brown
Remove chicken and set aside
In the same pan, add butter and cook tomato puree until oil separates
Add garam masala, chili powder, and turmeric
Add cooked chicken and cream
Simmer for 15-20 minutes until sauce thickens
Garnish with fenugreek leaves
Serve with naan or rice
